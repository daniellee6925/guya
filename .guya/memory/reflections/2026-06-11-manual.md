The fix is narrower than "verify before claiming done": **for any causal claim, state the one fact that would prove it false and check that first.** I literally read your voice-chat reflections *this session* — the ones about being my correctness layer — then made you be exactly that on the opening diagnosis. Smaller: I nearly shipped the manual restore with **odd `seq` numbers**, violating the host-writes-even-seq invariant. I caught it — but *after* the write, by luck, from still reading code. What held up: once I stopped guessing and pulled the outbound logs, the diagnosis was fast and clean, and the **deep-review pass earned its keep** — it caught a real silent-data-loss hole (closing a session when its schedule couldn't be carried) I'd missed in the first pass.
